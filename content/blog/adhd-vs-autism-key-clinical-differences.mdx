## Introduction

People love a crisp comparison table. Brains, unfortunately, are not crisp. ADHD and autism can co-occur, and they can also mimic each other in everyday behaviour—especially when anxiety, trauma, sleep deprivation, or learning differences are present.

## Attention differences vs social communication patterns

ADHD profiles often include variability in sustained attention, organisation, and impulse regulation—sometimes described as “time blindness” or difficulty switching tasks.

Autism profiles often include differences in social communication nuance, sensory processing, and flexible thinking—sometimes described as “getting stuck” or needing clearer expectations.

## Why overlap is not “contradictory”

A person can need support for sensory overwhelm **and** struggle with working memory. A person can communicate eloquently in writing **and** find spoken conversation draining.

## What a good assessment adds

A good assessment does not only name a pattern—it explains **mechanisms** and **supports**. If you are unsure where to start, begin with a team that listens slowly.
