## Introduction

Waiting is hard—especially when your child is struggling now, or when you have spent years wondering about your own brain. News coverage can feel alarming, but decisions are easier when you separate **facts**, **values**, and **what you can control**.

## Why waiting lists exist

Demand for neurodevelopmental assessments has grown quickly. Services are working to expand capacity, but training clinicians and safeguarding quality takes time. Waiting does not mean “nothing is wrong,” and it also does not mean you must accept harmful delay without options.

## Private assessments: quality questions to ask

If you explore private care, keep the conversation grounded:

- Who conducts the assessment, and what are their registered qualifications?
- How is information gathered beyond questionnaires?
- What does the report include, and how can it support school or workplace adjustments?

## A balanced path forward

Some families mix NHS and private touchpoints. Whatever route you choose, you deserve clarity, respect, and a plan that fits your real life.
