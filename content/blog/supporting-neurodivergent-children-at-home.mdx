## Introduction

Home is not school, and it should not have to perform like a clinic. Still, many families notice that a little **predictability** and a little **sensory thoughtfulness** can change the emotional temperature of a whole week.

## Creating a structured home without rigidity

Structure is not the same as control. Useful structure often looks like:

- Visual timetables for recurring transitions (not every moment)
- “First / then” language that is short and kind
- A calm-down corner that is framed as normal, not punishment

## Co-regulation comes first

When adults stay steadier, children recover faster. That is not blame—it is biology. If you can, build tiny recovery windows for yourself too: water, air, five minutes of quiet, a text to someone who gets it.

## When extra support helps

If meltdowns are frequent, sleep is persistently difficult, or school is asking for clarity, a professional assessment can be a bridge—not a verdict on your parenting.
