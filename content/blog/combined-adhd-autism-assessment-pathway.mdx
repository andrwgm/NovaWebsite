## Introduction

Overlap between ADHD and autism is common enough that it should be expected, not treated as unusual. The goal of a combined pathway is simple: **one story**, informed by evidence, that explains strengths and difficulties together.

## Why overlap matters clinically

Some “ADHD-like” behaviours can be driven by sensory overload, communication fatigue, or anxiety. Some “autism-like” preferences for structure can be amplified when attention regulation is hard. A careful history helps clinicians avoid treating symptoms in isolation.

## What families often find helpful

- A clear timeline of development and concerns
- Examples from school/work and home (not only clinic behaviour)
- Permission to ask questions about how conclusions were reached

## Next steps

If you are considering an assessment, bring questions. A strong team will welcome them—and will help you understand options without pressure.
